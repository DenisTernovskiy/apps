Privacy policy

These are personal applications used only by their author.

  • They access only the author's own accounts and files, with the author's explicit authorisation.
  • Data is read to copy it to the author's own computers. It is never sold, shared or sent to any third party.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising of any kind.
  • No accounts are offered to other people, and no data belonging to other people is processed.
  • Authorisation can be revoked at any time in Google Account settings.
